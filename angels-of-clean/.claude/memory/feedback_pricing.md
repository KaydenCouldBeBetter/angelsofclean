name: Pricing is quote-based
description: Do not implement automated pricing logic — pricing depends on equipment/resources evaluated by a manager
type: feedback
---

Do not build automated price calculation features.

**Why:** Pricing is done on a quote basis and depends on the equipment and resources expected to do the job, evaluated by a manager. The static "From $X" prices shown are intentionally starting estimates, not calculated totals.

**How to apply:** When working on the booking flow, leave pricing as static "starting from" labels. Don't add price calculators, dynamic totals, or pricing formulas.
